hold = pause
Try it: tap the right side for the next hour, the left side to go back, press and hold to pause.
  • The MP4 is still one video (saved to Drive like now); the hour grid is burned in. Hour boundaries are saved with it, so the app's viewer can jump hour by hour.
  • Grid follows who recorded: 1 → full screen, 2–3 → stacked, 4 → 2×2, 5–6 → 2×3, 7–9 → 3×3. People who skipped the hour aren't shown.
  • Each tile keeps the name and caption, at the dragged spot and in the chosen font.
  • Sound: everyone in the hour, mixed together.
