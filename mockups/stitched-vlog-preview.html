What the midnight stitch produces: every clip of the day, 3 seconds each, in hour order, with the label and caption burned in.

How the real file is built
  • 720×1280 MP4, 30 fps. Each clip is cut to its 3 seconds and played back to back, earliest hour first; clips in the same hour go in recording order.
  • Top-left: name · hour, white on a dark box.
  • The caption sits where it was dragged on the capture screen, in the font picked there (Classic, Bold, Elegant or Mono) and its colour.
  • Square or landscape clips are centred over a blurred copy of themselves, so there are never black bars (see the 1 PM clip).
  • Original sound is kept; clips without sound get silence.
