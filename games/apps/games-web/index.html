How it works

Your whole day, together, without anyone filming a whole day.

  1. ⏰1

    A nudge every hour

    Everyone in your group gets the same reminder, so the clips line up hour by hour.

  2. 🎥2

    Record 3 seconds

    Point, tap, done. Drop a caption on it if you like.

  3. 🎬3

    Watch yesterday's vlog

    At midnight the day is stitched together: one screen per hour, everyone playing at once.
